# 🇳🇪 Niger Marketplace

**A multi-user e-commerce platform designed for the Nigerien market.**

Niger Marketplace is a web application project intended to connect customers and sellers through a digital marketplace adapted to local shopping and delivery needs.

The project is being developed with a focus on reusable React components, clear application architecture, and a foundation for future backend integration.

> **Project status:** In development. The frontend is being built incrementally; backend integration and production readiness remain future steps.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Provide a convenient online shopping experience.
- Enable sellers to manage their products and shop activities.
- Support administrative workflows for marketplace management.
- Adapt the shopping experience to local pricing and delivery requirements in Niger.

## ✨ Planned Features

### Customer
- Browse products and view product details.
- Search for products.
- Manage a shopping cart.
- Complete checkout workflows.
- Track orders.

### Seller
- View a seller dashboard.
- Review sales and product information.
- Manage wallet and withdrawal workflows.

### Administrator
- Review shop registration requests.
- Approve or reject pending shops.
- Support marketplace administration.

## 🇳🇪 Local Market Focus

The application is designed with local marketplace requirements in mind, including:

- Prices displayed in FCFA.
- Delivery options adapted to local neighborhoods.
- Seller and customer workflows.
- Potential integration with local payment methods.
